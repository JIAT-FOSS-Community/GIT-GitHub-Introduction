# 🪄 Contributing to Open Sourcery

Welcome, Sorcerer! ✨ We are thrilled that you've been summoned to contribute to our collaborative guild. By participating in this project, you're joining a community of learners and mentors from the JIAT FOSS Community, supported by FOSS.LK and the Java Institute for Advanced Technology. 

## 📜 Guild Guidelines

We want this melting pot of ideas to be welcoming and empowering for everyone. 

- **Be Respectful:** Treat your fellow sorcerers with kindness. We're all here to learn and brew something amazing together. 🧪
- **Collaborate:** Share your knowledge, ask questions, and help others when they get stuck. ⚔️

## 🌿 How to Contribute

1. **Fork the Repository:** Create your own branch of the magic. 
2. **Make your Changes:** Whether it's fixing a typo, adding a new spell (feature), or brewing up some documentation, every little bit helps! 🔮
3. **Commit with Care:** Write clear, concise commit messages explaining what you've done. 
4. **Submit a Pull Request:** Send your changes back to the main cauldron for review. 

## ❓ Need Help? 

If you get stuck or have any questions, don't hesitate to reach out! You can open an issue or connect with us on our Discord/WhatsApp channels. We're here to help you cast your best code! 🧙‍♂️
